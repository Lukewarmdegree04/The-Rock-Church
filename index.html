Welcome Home

Where Godly passion meets Godly purpose.

Our Mission

To know God and make Him known

  • Grow a intimate community that has a passionate heart for Jesus.
  • Create more leaders from the people in the community.
  • Undertake meaningful mission trips to spread The Gospel.

Our Vision

Love God, love people, and make disciples of the nations.

  • The Greatest Commandment:
    Love the Lord with all your heart.
    Matthew 22:36-40
  • The Second Greatest Commandment:
    Love others as yourself.
  • The Great Commission:
    Go make disciples of all nations.
    Matthew 28:19

Our Core Values

Extending Beyond Ourselves

We trust God's power in everyday life and Him enabling us to reach out to the nations.


Jesus at the Center

We value Jesus being the center of everything we do in and around our church.




The Scripture

We use The Bible as our ultimate guide. We add nothing to it, and remove nothing from it.





Teamwork and Servant-Leadership

Everyone is empowered to play a role in The Kingdom because every person is valued as a friend and recognized as part of the priesthood of all believers.
